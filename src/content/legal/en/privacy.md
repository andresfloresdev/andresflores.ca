## 1. Who I am

This is the website of Andrés Flores, in Montreal. This policy explains what I do with personal information related to this site, under Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25) and PIPEDA.

## 2. Person in charge

The person in charge of protecting personal information is Andrés Flores. You can write to him at info@andresflores.ca with any question or request.

## 3. What the site collects

Nothing on its own. The site has no form, no analytics cookie, no advertising pixel and no tracking tool. If that changes, this policy will be updated and your consent will be asked before anything loads.

Email links open your email app. If you write to me, I receive what you choose to send, as an ordinary email.

## 4. Why I use it

To read your message and reply. I do not use it for any other purpose without your consent. I do not sell or rent it.

## 5. Where it goes

Your emails are kept in my mailbox. My email provider may host that data outside Quebec.

The site is served by a host that may keep technical logs (IP address, requested page) for a short time, for security.

## 6. How long I keep it

As long as needed to reply and follow up on our exchange. You can ask for its deletion at any time.

## 7. Your rights

Under Law 25, you can ask to access your information, have it corrected, withdraw your consent or have it deleted. You can also file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec. To exercise any of these rights, write to info@andresflores.ca.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, the last-updated date changes too.
